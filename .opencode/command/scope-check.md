Review the current work in progress and compare it to the original task/plan. Check for:

1. **Scope Creep**: Are we doing more than originally asked?
2. **Missing Items**: Are we missing any requirements from the original task?
3. **Tangents**: Have we gone off on tangents that don't serve the goal?
4. **Completeness**: Is the current approach fully addressing the problem?

Report back with:
- What's in scope and on track
- What's out of scope and should be stopped
- What's missing and needs attention
